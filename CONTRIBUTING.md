# Factual corrections

For a public factual correction, identify the document, edition/date, section and a reliable public source. Separate corrections to current product information from comments on historical material.

Use the project's [contact page](https://calorietoken.net/index.php/contact/) or a repository issue when available. Do not include private user data, signatures, credentials, wallet secrets, unpublished legal documents or private correspondence in a public issue.

Proposed corrections are reviewed before publication. Submission does not promise acceptance, a reward, a partnership or a release date. Keep original third-party attributions and identify any rights or permission needed for proposed new material.
