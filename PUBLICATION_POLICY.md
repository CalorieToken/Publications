# Publication and version policy

- The stable public file is `whitepaper/CalorieToken-Whitepaper.pdf`.
- Every public revision also receives a dated immutable copy under `releases/YYYY-MM-DD/`.
- Editable working sources are retained separately; the PDF is the public reading copy. Publishing a working source requires a separate publication decision.
- `SHA256SUMS` is regenerated for every frozen release.
- Current, historical and future material must remain explicitly separated.
- No draft, confidential showcase, wallet/fund record, raw KYC, credential or private evidence belongs in this repository.
- Existing public website PDF URLs should redirect to the stable canonical PDF instead of breaking.
- A redirect starts as HTTP 302 during validation and changes to HTTP 301 only after signed-out desktop/mobile checks pass.

## Roadmap and source history

- The current detailed roadmap is `roadmap/README.md`; a dated Markdown copy accompanies each published revision.
- The current website summary remains at `/index.php/roadmap/`.
- Historical PDFs retain their original content. Explain superseded claims in their archive index, rather than rewriting the historical document.
- A filename is not reliable evidence of the edition: record the date and version printed inside the document.
- A prepared draft is not described as a past public release. The local v4.0 preparation is therefore not listed as a published edition.
- New public releases may add verified historical editions without changing already archived bytes.
- Record factual corrections in `CHANGELOG.md`. Do not rewrite an earlier dated release to conceal a correction.

## Public and private material

Public material may include approved whitepapers, roadmaps, release notes, public project facts and rights notices. Signed contracts, KYC documents, signatures, private correspondence, unreleased showcases, detailed incident evidence and personal records belong outside this public repository. A factual public summary may link to an appropriate public register without exposing the underlying private dossier.

## Rights and claims

Preserve existing licences and attributions. Do not apply a new blanket licence to historical artwork, contributed media, external logos or third-party data. Regulatory notifications and trade-mark registrations are different matters; neither a GitHub upload nor a trade-mark entry proves permission to offer a regulated service.

## Link preservation

Maintain an exact old-to-new mapping for changed pages and downloads. Preserve existing page slugs, image URLs and anchors where feasible. Create a useful successor for retired content; do not redirect all missing pages to the home page. Keep historical reading paths available, and activate redirects only after the intended target exists. See [official links and preservation](links/README.md).
